### ⚠️ Toll Roads in Los Angeles — What You Need to Know

#### Metro ExpressLanes (I-10 & I-110 only)
These are the **only toll roads relevant to central LA**. They are optional express lanes on the far left of these two freeways — the regular lanes are always free.

| | |
|-|-|
| **Where** | I-10 (Santa Monica Freeway) and I-110 (Harbor Freeway) express lanes only |
| **Regular lanes** | ✅ Always free — just stay right |
| **Express lanes** | 💰 Toll applies — clearly marked on the left |
| **Payment method** | FasTrak transponder only — **no cash booths** |
| **No transponder penalty** | $9 admin fee + toll rate charged to rental company → passed to you |
| **Rental car surcharge** | Rental agencies add $15–30 admin fee on top of any toll |

#### Your Routes — Are They Affected?
| Route | Uses I-10 / I-110? | Toll Risk |
|-------|-------------------|-----------|
| LAX → Hollywood (via I-405 + Santa Monica Blvd) | ❌ No | ✅ Zero tolls |
| Hollywood → Parsons Pasadena (via US-101 + CA-134) | ❌ No | ✅ Zero tolls |

> **The Toll Roads** (SR-73, SR-133, SR-241, SR-261) are in Orange County — far south of your routes. Not relevant to your trip.

## Driving in California — License Requirements

### ✅ Your Canadian Driver's License is Valid in the USA

As a Canadian, you **do not need an International Driving Permit (IDP)** to drive in California or anywhere in the USA.

| | |
|-|-|
| **Canadian licence valid in USA?** | ✅ Yes — up to 6 months |
| **International Driving Permit needed?** | ❌ Not required for Canadians |
| **IDP recognised in California?** | ⚠️ California does not formally recognise IDP as a standalone licence |

### Official References
- 🇨🇦 **Government of Canada**: [International Driving Permit — travel.gc.ca](https://travel.gc.ca/travelling/documents/international-driving-permit) — confirms Canadian licence holders do not need an IDP in the USA
- 🇺🇸 **USAGov**: [Driving in the U.S. if you are not a citizen — usa.gov](https://www.usa.gov/non-citizen-driving) — confirms valid foreign licences are accepted

### ⚠️ One Thing to Check
Some **rental car companies** may ask for an IDP alongside your foreign licence. Since your Canadian licence is in English this is rarely enforced, but it's worth confirming with your chosen rental agency (e.g. Alamo, Budget, Enterprise) when booking.
